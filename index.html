Director's message

Dr. Akshaya Jain

Founder & Director, CIATN Institute · Founder, Skintillating

Aesthetic medicine today sits at the intersection of medical science, advanced technology and artistic precision. Over more than a decade in cosmetic and aesthetic medicine, Dr. Jain has watched the field evolve from basic cosmetic treatments into highly advanced, technology-driven clinical cosmetology.

She founded CIATN with a clear purpose: to build highly skilled, ethically grounded, clinically confident aesthetic professionals — through structured training, hands-on clinical exposure, advanced laser technology, and government-aligned skill development standards. The goal isn't just to prepare students to practise, but to lead.
